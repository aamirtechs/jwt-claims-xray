How it works

  • Decodes header and payload locally and humanises registered claims: exp, iat and nbf become local and UTC times with a live countdown.
  • Verifies HS256, RS256 and ES256 signatures with WebCrypto in the page. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Paste two tokens to get a claim-by-claim diff of what changed.
  • Redacted copy masks the signature and sensitive claims, safe to paste into a support ticket.
  • Works offline after load. Decoding is not verification, so always verify before trusting a token.
